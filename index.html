Burrow

Your photos, tucked away safe.

Burrow is a photo vault that hides your photos, videos and files behind a pattern you draw. Everything is encrypted on your phone. Each pattern opens its own vault, and nobody looking at the app can tell how many you have.

  • No ads.
  • No account.
  • Nothing leaves your phone.
Pricing: free while we're new. When Burrow Pro launches, adding new items will need Pro. Whatever you've already hidden stays yours: view, export and delete it free, always.

Help

I forgot my pattern

On the lock screen, tap Forgot your pattern? and type your 8-word recovery phrase. You'll then choose a new pattern. Without the phrase, nobody can open the vault, including us. There's no account and no server that could reset it.

I deleted photos from my gallery after hiding them. Are they really gone?

On iPhone, deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Empty that album in the Photos app too. On Android, Burrow uses the system's delete request.

Can I move my vault to a new phone?

Not yet. Your vault is tied to this phone's security chip. Encrypted backup is planned as a future feature.

Something isn't working

Email innovationapollo@gmail.com with your phone model and what happened. Please never send your pattern or recovery phrase. We will never ask for them.
